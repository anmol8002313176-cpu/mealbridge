Why food rescue matters

MealBridge helps donors and verified organisations coordinate surplus food pickup. For this demo, organisation verification and recipient delivery are not connected to a real-world service.

Important demo notes

This is a functional browser-based prototype. Listings are stored on this device using local storage. It does not currently perform real NGO verification, location-based distance calculations, SMS notifications, or secure server uploads. Do not upload sensitive personal information.
